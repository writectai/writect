FAQs

Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before you install.

Is Writect free to use?

Yes. Free includes the Chrome Extension, web app, selection tools, 10 AI actions per day (300 per month max), and Limited Page Assistant. Pro is $9.99/month with 3,000 AI actions/month (150/day) and Full Page Assistant.

What counts as an AI action?

Most tools use 1 AI action. Long writing uses 2. Page Assistant uses 3. Very large pages or requests use 5. Limits reset daily and monthly (UTC).

How do I cancel Pro?

Open the web app → Settings → Manage billing. You can cancel anytime; access continues until the end of the billing period.

Where can I use Writect?

On Chromium browsers (Chrome, Edge, and similar) — highlight text on Gmail, Docs, LinkedIn, WhatsApp Web, and more, or use Page Assistant from the side panel.
