Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Writect uses a small amount of browser storage to make the product work.

What we use

  • Essential session data — to keep you signed in on the web app.
  • Preferences — such as theme (light/dark) stored in localStorage.
  • Extension storage — the Chrome extension stores your session and settings locally on your device.

Analytics cookies

We do not currently use third-party advertising cookies on the marketing site.

Your choices

You can clear site data in your browser settings, or sign out to remove the web session. Extension data can be cleared by removing the extension or clearing its storage.
