Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Writect (“we”) provides an AI writing Chrome extension and web app. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account info from Google Sign-In (name, email, avatar).
  • Usage data needed to run the product (AI Actions counts, plan, billing status).
  • Text you submit for AI features, processed to return a result.
  • Technical logs (errors, request metadata) to keep the service reliable.

How we use data

We use your data to provide Writect features, enforce Free/Pro limits, process payments via Stripe, and improve reliability. We do not sell your personal data.

AI providers

To generate replies, we send the text you submit to third-party AI providers (such as OpenAI and Google Gemini) under our API accounts. We do not use your writing to train Writect’s own models. Provider handling of data is also governed by their terms and settings.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to accounts uses Google Sign-In.

Questions about privacy: Contact & Support.
