Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

At Writect AI, your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we need it, and how it is handled when you use Writect.

This policy applies to Writect AI, including our website at writect.ai, the Writect Chrome Extension, and related services. Writect AI is operated by Websrow FZE.

By using Writect, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information reasonably needed to provide, secure, maintain, and improve Writect.

Account Information

When you create a Writect account, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Account ID
  • Profile picture, if you sign in with Google
  • Account preferences
  • Subscription and plan information

You can create an account using Google Sign-In or by signing up directly with your email and password.

Text You Use With Writect

When you use Writect’s AI features, you may provide text for Writect to process. This can include text you ask Writect to fix, rephrase, translate, summarize, explain, shorten, expand, change in tone, or otherwise improve.

Writect does not permanently store your prompts, selected text, webpage content, or AI-generated responses in our Database. Your content is processed for the request and may be held briefly in server memory while the request is being completed.

The content needed to complete your request is sent to the selected AI provider, such as OpenAI or Google Gemini.

2. Web App Chat History

Writect web app chat history is stored locally in your browser and is not stored in Writect’s database.

Because this history is stored on your device, it may be lost if you clear your browser or site data, use a different browser, or switch to another device. Writect currently does not provide multi-device synchronization of this locally stored chat history.

3. Writect Chrome Extension

The Writect Chrome Extension lets you use Writect’s AI writing tools while browsing the web.

When you choose to use a Writect feature, the extension may process selected text, text you enter into Writect, and content needed to perform the action you requested. For features such as Page Assistant, relevant webpage content may also need to be processed.

Installing the extension does not mean Writect permanently stores everything you browse or type online. Writing content used for an AI request is processed to provide the requested feature and is not permanently saved in our database.

4. Page Assistant

Page Assistant helps you understand and work with content on the webpage you are viewing. When you use Page Assistant, Writect may process relevant webpage content to provide functions such as summarizing, explaining, answering questions, or working with page content.

Content processed through Page Assistant is not permanently stored in Writect’s Database. It may be temporarily processed by our API and the selected AI provider to complete your request.

5. Account and Usage Information

Writect stores account and usage information needed to operate the service. This may include:

  • Name and email address
  • Account and authentication information
  • Plan and subscription status
  • Stripe customer or subscription identifiers
  • AI action counts and usage information
  • AI model used
  • Token counts and related technical usage information
  • Technical or diagnostic information needed to operate and secure the service

We use this information to manage accounts and plan limits, process subscriptions, monitor service performance, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, maintain security, and improve Writect.

6. How We Use Your Information

We use information collected through Writect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate and sign you in
  • Provide Writect’s AI features
  • Process the AI actions you request
  • Manage Free and Pro plans and usage limits
  • Process subscriptions and payments
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain and improve Writect
  • Detect fraud, misuse, and abuse
  • Protect Writect and our users
  • Fix bugs and technical issues
  • Send important account, security, payment, or service messages
  • Meet applicable legal requirements

7. AI Service Providers

Writect uses third-party artificial intelligence providers to generate AI responses. Our current AI providers include OpenAI and Google Gemini.

When you use an AI feature, the content necessary to complete your request is sent to the relevant AI provider. For example, if you ask Writect to rephrase a paragraph, that paragraph must be processed by the AI provider so the requested result can be generated.

Writect does not train its own AI models on your writing.

Although Writect does not permanently store your writing content in its own database, third-party AI providers process information under their own applicable terms, privacy policies, contractual arrangements, and data-retention practices.

8. Google Sign-In

Writect offers Sign in with Google as one way to create or access your account. If you choose Google Sign-In, Google may provide us with basic account information such as your name, email address, profile picture, and Google account identifier.

We use this information to authenticate you and manage your Writect account. Writect currently uses Google for account authentication only. Signing in with Google does not give Writect access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Google Calendar, or other private Google Workspace content.

If Writect introduces features in the future that require additional Google permissions, we will explain the requested access and update this Privacy Policy where necessary.

9. Email and Password Sign-Up

You may also create a Writect account using your email address and password. We use this information to create and authenticate your account and provide account-related services.

We use appropriate security practices for authentication credentials. You are responsible for keeping your login details confidential and should contact us if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

10. Payments and Stripe

Payments for paid Writect plans are processed through Stripe. Stripe may collect information needed to process your payment, such as your name, billing information, payment card details, and transaction information.

Writect may receive limited payment-related information from Stripe, including subscription status, plan purchased, payment status, transaction or customer identifiers, and subscription dates.

Writect does not need to store your full payment card details when those details are handled directly by Stripe. Stripe processes payment information according to its own terms and privacy practices.

12. Cookies and Local Browser Storage

Writect may use cookies, local storage, and similar browser technologies to make the service work properly.

These technologies may be used to keep you signed in, maintain sessions, remember preferences, improve security, store web app chat history locally on your device, and improve the operation of Writect.

You can control browser storage through your browser settings. Clearing cookies, local storage, or site data may remove locally stored information, including Writect web app chat history.

13. When We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We only share information when it is necessary to provide and operate Writect, comply with the law, protect our services and users, or fulfill our business obligations.

We may share limited information with trusted service providers that help us operate Writect, including:

  • AI providers, such as OpenAI and Google Gemini, to process AI requests;
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers, such as Render;
  • Payment providers, such as Stripe;
  • Authentication, email, security, monitoring, and other technical service providers we may use to operate Writect.

These providers receive only the information reasonably necessary to provide their services to us, subject to their applicable terms, privacy practices, and our contractual arrangements where applicable.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, court order, governmental request, or other valid legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect Writect, Websrow FZE, our users, or others from fraud, abuse, security threats, or illegal activity.

If Writect or Websrow FZE is involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, financing, or sale of all or part of the business, information may be transferred as part of that transaction as permitted by applicable law.

14. We Do Not Sell Personal Information

Writect does not sell your personal information for money. We also do not sell the writing content you submit to Writect to advertisers or data brokers.

If our practices change in a way that affects your privacy rights, we will update this Privacy Policy and provide any notices or choices required by applicable law.

15. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure.

No website, browser extension, database, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. If you believe your Writect account or personal information has been compromised, contact us at support@writect.ai.

16. How Long We Keep Information

We keep account and usage information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected and to operate Writect.

Some information may need to be retained longer for security, fraud prevention, payment and accounting records, legal obligations, resolving disputes, or enforcing agreements.

Writect does not permanently store AI prompts, selected text, webpage content, or generated AI responses in its database. Web app chat history is stored locally on the user’s device.

17. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Writect account and associated personal information by contacting us at support@writect.ai.

When we receive a valid deletion request, we will delete or anonymize applicable personal information unless we are required or permitted to retain certain information for legal, security, fraud prevention, payment, accounting, or dispute-resolution purposes.
