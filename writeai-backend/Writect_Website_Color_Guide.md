# Writect Website Color & UI Guide

**Clean • Fresh • Natural • Easy on the Eyes**

> Use the Writect logo colors as accents rather than filling the entire website with strong pink and purple. The website should be predominantly light, with dark purple for structure and pink for actions. The goal is a calm, premium SaaS/AI-product feel with plenty of breathing space.

---

## Color Tokens

| Role | Name | Hex | Recommended Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main brand pink | Bright Magenta | `#ED268F` | Primary CTAs, links, active states, highlights, small accents |
| Secondary pink | Soft Magenta | `#D91F78` | Hover states, secondary pink accents |
| Deep brand purple | Plum Purple | `#701951` | Secondary buttons, headings, icons, brand elements |
| Dark purple | Deep Purple | `#370F44` | Major headings, dark sections, footer, premium areas |
| Main background | Very Soft Lavender | `#FCF9FC` | Main page background |
| Section background | Light Pink-Lavender | `#F8F2F7` | Alternating sections and subtle visual separation |
| Cards | White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, panels, pricing cards, UI containers |
| Main text | Dark Charcoal-Purple | `#29212B` | Body text and navigation |
| Secondary text | Muted Purple-Gray | `#6F6672` | Descriptions, helper text, metadata |
| Borders | Very Light Purple | `#EDE5EC` | Card borders, dividers, input borders |
| Success | Soft Green | `#3FA77A` | Positive/success states |

---

## 1. Overall Color Balance

| Share | Color | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 60% | `#FFFFFF` / `#FCF9FC` | Light background / breathing space |
| 20% | `#F8F2F7` | Soft alternate sections |
| 10% | `#29212B` / `#6F6672` | Text |
| 5% | `#701951` / `#370F44` | Brand structure / headings / dark areas |
| 5% | `#ED268F` | Actions and highlights |

> **Important:** Do not use dark purple or bright pink as the default page background. Large areas of saturated color should be rare.

---

## 2. Header & Navigation

- **Header background:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Navigation text:** `#29212B`
- **Primary CTA:** `#ED268F` with white text
- **CTA hover:** `#D91F78`

Keep the header clean and mostly neutral. The logo should remain the main brand identifier.

---

## 3. Hero Section

- **Background:** `#FCF9FC`
- **Main heading:** `#370F44`
- **Body copy:** `#6F6672`
- **Highlighted word/phrase:** `#ED268F`

> Do not make the entire hero dark purple. Use the light background to create an airy, modern first impression.

---

## 4. Buttons & Calls to Action

| Button Type | Background | Text | Border / Hover |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary CTA | `#ED268F` | `#FFFFFF` | Hover: `#D91F78` |
| Secondary CTA | `#FFFFFF` | `#701951` | Border: `#701951` |

> **Rule:** Pink should communicate action — *Start, Get Started, Apply, Upgrade, Submit*, etc. Do not use pink for large paragraphs or every UI element.

---

## 5. Cards, Features & UI

- **Card background:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Card border:** `#EDE5EC`
- **Card heading:** `#370F44`
- **Description:** `#6F6672`
- **Icons / small accents:** `#ED268F`

> Avoid making every card purple or pink. White cards on light backgrounds will keep the interface clean and readable.

---

## 6. Section Backgrounds

| Type | Color |
|---|---|
| Normal | `#FCF9FC` |
| Alternate | `#F8F2F7` |
| Card | `#FFFFFF` |

A simple rhythm such as **light → white → soft lavender → white** creates separation without visual pressure.

---

## 7. Dark Purple Sections

Use `#370F44` selectively for a major feature/CTA section and the footer. Use white text and `#ED268F` for the CTA. One or two strong dark sections are enough.

---

## 8. Footer

- **Background:** `#370F44`
- **Main text:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Secondary text:** `#D8C8D5`
- **Links:** `#F4B4D2`
- **CTA:** `#ED268F`

---

## 9. Gradients

Use the signature gradient `#ED268F → #701951` only occasionally — for hero artwork, special visuals, decorative shapes, or a premium highlight. Do not apply gradients to every button, card, or section.

---

## 10. Typography & Readability

- Avoid pure black (`#000000`) for normal website text.
- Use `#29212B` for primary text and `#6F6672` for secondary text.
- Keep sufficient line spacing and generous padding around sections, cards, and buttons.
- The design should feel **spacious** rather than crowded.

## Design Principle

**Light background + dark purple headings + charcoal text + pink CTAs + lots of white space + subtle lavender sections + occasional deep-purple sections.**

The Writect logo supplies the personality. The website should supply the breathing space. The final result should feel **clean, fresh, natural, modern, premium** and comfortable to view for long periods.

> **Developer note:** Treat these hex values as the core design tokens. Keep saturated colors limited to purposeful UI elements so the brand remains recognizable without making the interface visually heavy.
